# LocalLink

Send photos, files, and text between your Windows PC and your iPhone — over your own Wi‑Fi. No internet, no accounts, nothing leaves your home network.

## ⬇️ Download

**[👉 Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**

Download the installer (`LocalLink-Setup-*.exe`) from the latest release, then follow the steps below.

## Setup (2 minutes)

1. **Install on your PC** — run the installer.
   *Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” screen — that’s normal for small apps. Click **More info → Run anyway**, then **Yes**.*
2. **Connect your iPhone** — open the **Camera**, scan the **QR code** LocalLink shows, then tap **Approve** on the PC.
3. **Add to Home Screen** — LocalLink shows a short guide in Safari. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

> Your phone and PC must be on the **same Wi‑Fi**.

## Updating

When a new version is posted here, just download the new installer and run it over the old one. Your paired phone stays connected.

---

🔒 **Private by design.** LocalLink is local‑only — files go straight from your PC to your phone over Wi‑Fi and never touch the internet or any server.
