How it works

1Install LocalLink on your PC. It sits quietly in the system tray.
2Scan the QR code with your iPhone camera. No App Store, no login.
3Send a file — it appears on your phone in seconds. Three easy ways, shown below.

Why people like it

  • Nothing leaves your network

    Files go straight from your PC to your phone. There’s no server, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Full original quality

    Photos and videos arrive exactly as they were — nothing gets compressed or re‑encoded.

  • No account

    No email, no password, no subscription. Install it and it works.

  • Both ways, any size

    PC to phone or phone to PC — including big 4K videos and whole folders.
